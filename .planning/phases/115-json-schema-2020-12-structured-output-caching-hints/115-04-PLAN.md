<objective>
Ship SCHM-02 as what it measurably is: a sibling constructor, an era statement in rustdoc, and tests
that prove the existing permissiveness is deliberate.

Purpose: D-05 asked for the "2.15 object-only bridge" to be LOCATED before being relaxed.
115-RESEARCH § Finding 6 located it — **it is not in pmcp's code.** `structured_content` is already
`Option<Value>` (`src/types/tools.rs:565`); both dispatchers (`core.rs:823`, `mod.rs:2215`) hand the
handler's `serde_json::Value` straight through with no shape check; a repo-wide `is_object()` grep
returns zero hits on any structured-content path. The object-only constraint lived in the **v1 spec
text** (`structuredContent?: { [key: string]: unknown }` plus *"Currently restricted to `type:
"object"` at the root level"* on `outputSchema`), and the 2026-07-28 schema removes both
(`structuredContent?: unknown`).

So there is **no guard to remove and no derivation to change**. The executor must NOT go looking for
a bridge to dismantle. What ships is D-06's sibling constructor, the era rustdoc, and the tests that
turn today's accidental permissiveness into a pinned contract.

Output: `CallToolResult::structured_value`, era rustdoc on `structured_content` and on both
constructors, and non-object coverage in `tests/structured_tool_output.rs` across both dispatchers.

**Why `depends_on: ["115-02"]` when nothing here touches the wire:** 115-02 captures the
pre-change v1 golden bytes, and D-13 is explicit that once any field lands they are unrecoverable.
Nothing in this plan changes a wire byte, so the ordering is discipline rather than necessity — but
the discipline is cheap and the alternative is a golden capture taken on a partially-changed tree.
</objective>
