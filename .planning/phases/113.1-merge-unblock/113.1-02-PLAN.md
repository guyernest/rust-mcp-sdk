<objective>
Close D-113-R: `SseParser::drain_complete_lines` restarts its `find('\n')` search at offset 0 on
every `feed()` call, so a peer that chooses 1-byte chunk framing gets one full-buffer rescan per
byte. Measured at 833 ms of CPU for 256 KiB of peer input in release, 2015 ms in debug — quadratic,
so 1 MiB is roughly 13 s. Both incremental feeders (`connect_sse` at `src/shared/http.rs:371-378`
and the listen client at `src/client/subscriptions.rs:248-255`) call `feed` once per peer-chosen
frame, so the framing is entirely the attacker's to pick.

Purpose: this is HTTP-09's clause 3 — *"no scan over peer-chosen input is worse than O(n)"* — and it
is the reason HTTP-09 stands at `[ ]` on the merits. It is a remote CPU-exhaustion channel, not a
tidiness issue.

are ONE atomic change — see Task 2), four new falsifiable guards, an amended rustdoc on the existing
weak guard, and a recorded fuzz campaign.

**Edit 1 — the cursor (D-12).** Capture `let scan_start = self.buffer.len();` **immediately before**
`self.buffer.push_str(data);`. Introduce `let mut search_from = scan_start;` alongside the existing

and compute `let line_end = search_from + offset;`. At the bottom of each iteration, set both
`consumed = line_end + 1;` and `search_from = consumed;`.

**`consumed` MUST stay initialised to 0.** It drives the final `self.buffer.drain(..consumed)` and the
CRLF lookback guard. Seeding it to `scan_start` would break the case RESEARCH pins: when the retained
prefix ends with `\r` and the new chunk begins with `\n`, `line_end == scan_start` and `line_end - 1`
points INTO the retained prefix; the guard `line_end > consumed` is then `scan_start > 0`, which is
true, so the `\r` is correctly trimmed. With `consumed` seeded to `scan_start` that guard would be
false and the `\r` would leak into the line.

Everything else in the loop is unchanged and must stay so: `line_end` remains a BYTE index; the CRLF
lookback stays `self.buffer.as_bytes()[line_end - 1] == b'\r'` and never `chars().nth(...)` (that was
the remote-panic bug T-113-67); the guard stays `line_end > consumed`, not `> 0` and not
`> search_from`. The final `self.buffer.drain(..consumed)` is unchanged.

**Edit 2 — delete the `debug_assert` (D-15).** Remove the whole `debug_assert!(...)` statement at

it starts empty; (b) why that invariant is now the SOUNDNESS PRECONDITION for `scan_start` — no `'\n'`

assertion was deleted rather than kept — it was itself an O(len) scan per call and kept the function

`feed_complete_body`'s precondition-block convention at `:441-460`.
