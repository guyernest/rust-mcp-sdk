<objective>
Land the only verification the SDK can perform offline — the subject-digest comparison — at BOTH ends, and make its failure mode a value on the way out and a non-zero exit at the CLI.

D-02: `pack_server` computes the would-be UNATTESTED manifest digest and refuses to pack when the supplied subject differs, making "attestation attached to the wrong package" unrepresentable the way pack-time placeholder validation made a bad config-slot declaration unrepresentable in 120-05. `unpack_server` re-derives it independently, because neither end may assume the other ran — the attestation arrives from the platform, not from this repo.

D-03: on unpack a mismatch is DATA, not an error, deliberately unlike `digest::verify`'s fail-closed behaviour and unlike the V6 rule recorded at `cargo-pmcp/src/commands/package/inspect.rs:8`. The distinction must be written down where both live.

D-06: `inspect` renders the full diagnostic and then exits non-zero, so the mismatch is gateable in CI without parsing stdout, and a human at a terminal loses nothing.

Purpose: give the phrase "a verification path exists" a precise, honest meaning — subject comparison, nothing more.
Output: two pack-time gate functions, an unpack-side verdict, one new error variant, and the three-state `inspect` contract.
</objective>

| pmcp.run (attestation issuer) -> `pack_server` caller | The subject digest is supplied by an external party and is checked, not trusted |
