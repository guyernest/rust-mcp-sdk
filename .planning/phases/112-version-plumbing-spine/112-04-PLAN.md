<objective>
Wire the v2 opt-in accept-list into the builder and thread a per-request `ProtocolContext`, resolved once at ingress from `_meta`, through BOTH native dispatch sites plus the wasm mirror — the load-bearing "spine" of Phase 112. Also add the `extensions` builder convenience for VERS-08 population.

Purpose: This is where era-detection actually happens. The opt-in accept-list (D-01/D-02) makes v1-only the default (D-04) and dual expressible with one API; the ProtocolContext threading (VERS-01) makes era available to every handler. Wiring at both dispatch sites + wasm is the exact Phase-109-00 precedent (Pitfall 3).
Output: `.with_supported_protocol_versions()` + v1-only default stored on ServerCore; ingress ProtocolContext resolution (opted-in servers only); `.with_protocol_context()` in the extra chain at core.rs and mod.rs; wasm-mirror parity; `.with_extension()` builder convenience.
</objective>
